Privacy policy

This covers orders placed with us in the store, on our website, and through marketplaces such as eBay.

What we collect

For an order we keep your name, shipping address, the items you bought, and, for marketplace orders, your marketplace username and order number. Card payments are handled by our payment processor or the marketplace; we never see or store full card numbers.

How we use it

Only to fill, ship, and support your order, and to keep our inventory and accounting records. We do not sell, rent, or share your information for marketing.

Who else sees it

Our point-of-sale provider (Square), the marketplace you ordered through, and the shipping carrier, each only as needed to complete the order.

Deleting your data

If you close your eBay account, eBay tells us and we remove your personal details from our records. You can also ask us to delete your information at anotherroundsaltlake@gmail.com.
